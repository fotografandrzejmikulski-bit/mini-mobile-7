# Legal Gate — Poland

This document is a deployment gate, not legal advice.

Before any RF transmission, verify the current requirements published by the Polish regulator (UKE) for the exact band, bandwidth, location, power and use case.

## Private network

A private LTE/5G deployment is not automatically authorized merely because the equipment is privately owned. Spectrum use and radio operation remain subject to applicable authorization and technical conditions.

## Required checks

1. Current frequency-management conditions for the intended band.
2. Whether the deployment qualifies as own-use/private-network use or falls into another regulatory category.
3. Required radio permit or other authorization.
4. Permitted frequency block and bandwidth.
5. Location/area restrictions.
6. Transmit power, antenna and emission conditions.
7. Equipment conformity and applicable cybersecurity/communications requirements.
8. Numbering and interconnection rules if public telephony is later introduced.

## Public numbering

Do not assign a real Polish +48 public mobile number to a private subscriber by inventing a number. Public numbering and interconnection require the appropriate framework with the relevant operator/regulatory arrangements.

## RF stop condition

No physical RAN transmission is part of the software-only repository stage. Keep radio transmission disabled until all applicable requirements for the exact installation have been verified.
